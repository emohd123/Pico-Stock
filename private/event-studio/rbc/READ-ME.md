# Royal Bahrain Concours 2026 - editable scene handoff

Open `private/event-studio/rbc/Royal-Bahrain-Concours-2026.blend` in Blender 4.5 or newer. The file is self-contained: geometry and materials are embedded, with 931 scene objects, 253 preserved furniture placements, presentation cameras, and an editable 90-second camera animation. It includes the current ground plane, mapped course features and supplier-corrected MQ40 lounge tents. All six presentation stills were rebuilt from this revision.

Each tent is a named parent with separate roof, frame, walls, glazing and floor. Furniture is attached to its host tent and keeps its catalogue product ID. The original scene, calibration and furnishing records are embedded as Blender text blocks as well as included JSON files.

The browser scene uses metres and Y-up coordinates. Blender uses metres and Z-up: `(x, y, z)` becomes `(x, -z, y)`. Position means the floor/base origin. Named source plots A1-A27, S1-S19 and V1-V6 are preserved.

To rebuild a Blender file from a saved browser revision, retain this bundle's directory structure and run:

```powershell
blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/event-studio/import_layout.py -- --layout path/to/revision.json --output path/to/revision.blend
```

Use the model's custom properties to locate its source object ID, dimensions, measurement status and catalogue product reference. To edit one linked furniture instance without changing matching instances, make its mesh data single-user first. Export an edited furniture mesh to GLB at metre scale with its origin at floor centre, retaining its product ID for replacement in the catalogue.

The ground is no longer flat. The site falls 3.8 m from the clubhouse end down to the lake end, a gradient of 1 in 150 along its length, measured over 276 points on a 25 m grid across the event footprint. Two independent public elevation models agree on that fall within 6% (-6.48 and -6.89 mm per metre). They disagree on the direction of the cross fall, so the model carries none. After removing this plane the models still differ by 1.4-1.7 m RMS and correlate only 0.36, so local mounding, bunker depth and green contours are NOT modelled: a 30-90 m elevation model cannot resolve them, and SRTM was flown before this course was built. Surveyed levels or a drone survey would be needed for those. Tent floors are level; each tent stands on the ground at its own position and its furniture rides that floor. The exception is the four driving-range mounds, described below, which are modelled as turf domes.

The existing course inside the event footprint is drawn from OpenStreetMap: 14 bunkers, 7 putting greens, 6 teeing grounds, the fairway, the practice ground and 16 cart paths, clipped to the footprint and locked. They are placed by fitting this plan's lake to the real shoreline traced from Esri World Imagery, with rotation and scale free: the outlines agree to 0.7 m on average, and the same fit puts the two paved circles beside the Majlis within 0.2 m and 0.4 m of where the imagery shows them, without having been told about them. Plan +X points 0.49 degrees east of true north. The plan's drawing of the existing venue is about 1.4% smaller than the ground, so the course is placed against the venue as drawn. This replaces the September fit to the OpenStreetMap lake outline, which had slid everything about 12 m along the site. Horizontal accuracy is roughly 3 m and no depth is surveyed. That data is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Ten structures currently need ground works: the Oasis Club and five British Heritage tents stand on a bunker, S14 and S15 on a teeing ground, the East Toilets on a cart path, and the Coffee Bar on a driving-range mound. Five display cars stand on a mound. The studio flags any tent, stage or building on these, and any car on a mound.

The 2D drawing is the horizontal reference. All six explicit dimension spans agree with the chosen scale within 1.5 mm; dimension annotations are assumed to be millimetres. Rectangular footprint detection quantizes vector endpoints to 1.75 cm. Water and landscape curves are simplified within approximately 12.2 cm. Heights, tent roof sections, openings, materials, generic cars and tree geometry are editable visual estimates because construction sections were not provided. Site levels now follow the measured ground plane described above, within the limits stated there.

The 238 furniture placements across 28 zones are proposed arrangements. Catalogue stock was checked at creation; no stock was reserved. The studio offers only what the Pico Stock product catalogue lists, and takes each item's size and stock from its product record: where the record publishes dimensions, such as `H79*D47*W51cm`, the item is shown at exactly that size; the remainder keep an estimated measurement status until their records carry measurements.

The six 3840 x 2160 PNG renders are supplied as separate downloads and are not packed in the editable kit. The animated camera in Blender covers frames 1-2160 at 24 fps. The delivered browser walkthrough is a separate real-time recording.

To reproduce source extraction, install Python packages `pymupdf shapely numpy opencv-python pillow` and run `scripts/event-studio/extract_site.py` followed by `scripts/event-studio/furnish_seed.py`. The included `Source-Site-Plan.pdf` is used as the input. Re-extraction restores the source layout, so export any saved working revision before regenerating it.

The included `Studio-User-Guide.md` explains browser navigation, saved versions, stock guidance, render capture and the JSON exchange workflow.

`Royal-Concours-Editable-Kit.zip` contains the Blender file, all 99 furniture GLBs, scripts, source PDF, guides and JSON records. Presentation renders and the recorded walkthrough are separate downloads to keep this kit below the storage upload limit.

Venue refinement: the official Royal Golf Club photographs and the DP World Tour 2026 aerial-map inset corroborate the clubhouse and circular Majlis. The Majlis footprint is the original source vector 14482 (19.06577m diameter). Its 15m height, the main clubhouse 12m height, glazing, roof profile and narrow stone shoreline are editable photographic estimates. Existing source positions and footprints are retained. See `references/venue-research.md` and JSON for the evidence and confidence. The golf-tournament map is used only for permanent architecture.


Supplier update (24 September 2026): the four car club lounges now use open MQ40 tents at 10.5 × 12 m and 6.8 m peak. The 95 m² supplier area is rounded; the nominal polygon is 94.5 m². The browser offers the three-peaked 12 × 6 m Arabesque alternative, with estimated 5.5 m overall height, and an optional 12 m glass front. Rental rates: MQ40 BHD 2,900; Arabesque BHD 2,400; glass BHD 1,800 extra. Furniture and décor are excluded; flooring, VAT and rental period unspecified. Photo-based curvature, finials, bracing and anchoring are visual approximations. See supplier/tent-options-report.json and the two original images.


Supplier comparison library: `supplier/Lounge-Tent-Options.blend` contains three named reusable asset collections. `supplier/Lounge-Tent-Comparison.pdf` compares their matched-scale previews, footprint diagrams and rental rates. Library positions are for display only, not the event site. Each collection retains separate roof, frame, glazing and proposed floor meshes. Rebuild the library with `node --experimental-default-type=module scripts/event-studio/create-tent-library.mjs`, then run `blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/event-studio/build_tent_library.py` from this project root. The PDF generator uses ReportLab and Windows Arial/Georgia fonts.


Driving-range mounds (28 September 2026): Pico's overlay of the plan on Google Earth marks four turf mounds on the driving range. Each was measured on Esri World Imagery under the alignment above: 10-11 m across, placed to about 3 m, and each lands within 2.6-5.1 m of the overlay's circle. Google Earth's own imagery (9 August 2025) shows the same four. Their 1 m height is an estimate: target mounds usually rise 0.5-1.5 m, Google Earth reads terrain only in whole metres, and no level survey exists. They are locked course features named `venue-mound-1` to `-4`; cars on them sit tilted on their wheels.

Booth specification (30 September 2026): every 5 x 5 m pagoda booth (62 of them) follows Pico's elevation drawing. The frame rises 3.5 m on 400 mm corner posts, clad in RBC red (#de2826) at the front with a red header; the peak is about 5.55 m, read from the drawing's roof outline. A 4.2 x 1.0 m fascia graphic, lit by an LED strip, sits above a 4.2 m wide, 2.3 m high clear opening, and a 4.8 x 2.4 m backwall graphic hangs inside. The fascia carries the RBC 2026 logo, the booth's name and a logo space; set **Fascia name** in the inspector to show an exhibitor. Side walls and materials remain estimates. Brand colours come from the RBC 2026 brand guideline: red #de2826, navy #00263a, grey #535463.

The delivered `.blend` predates the October alignment, mounds, booth specification and roof frames. Rebuild it from a saved revision with `import_layout.py` as described above.

Tent roofs and light (October 2026): a pitched marquee spans its short side and is lengthened in 5 m bays, so a gable roof's ridge runs along the tent's longer side; set `metadata.ridge` to `"x"` or `"z"` on a tent to override that. This turns the Oasis Club into a 10 m span, 35 m long marquee and the Corporate Lounge into a 17 m span; the Owners Enclosure was already right. Inside, the frame is drawn under the fabric: a portal rafter at every bay, the ridge beam and, past a 15 m span, a purlin halfway up each slope; pagodas have four hip rafters to a hub and a king post, and the hexagonal pavilions a hip from each corner to the apex. Member spacing and sections follow standard frame-tent practice, not a supplier drawing. Every roof is built with its outer face outward whichever way its outline was traced; the gable ends of the Owners Enclosure and Corporate Lounge and all five hexagonal roofs had been inside out. The fabric lets light through, more where the sun falls on the outer face. Evening light puts the sun 12 degrees up in the west-south-west, lights the marquees from inside and the booth fascias from their LED strips.

Surroundings (October 2026): the ground for 1.1 x 0.84 km around and under the event is drawn as it is, from a half-metre classification of Esri World Imagery into turf, sand, water and paving, with OpenStreetMap roads and lakes painted in. 247 neighbouring buildings stand at their mapped footprints, from Microsoft's Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL) with OpenStreetMap filling gaps; heights are not mapped, so houses are drawn 7.5 m high. Where the event plan places a structure, the plan wins and no mapped building is drawn there. Rebuild with `python scripts/event-studio/build_ground_map.py`, then `node --experimental-default-type=module scripts/event-studio/apply-venue-survey.mjs` to version the files in the seed.
